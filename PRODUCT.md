# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Authorized GTP and SCPH organizers and scanner staff use this internal web app
at desks and on mobile devices to manage guest passes, check arrivals and review
attendance during the four-day conference.

## Product Purpose

Provide a dependable, fast and secure operational tool for guest check-in.
Staff must be able to understand data and complete time-sensitive actions
without ambiguity, duplicate submissions or unnecessary waiting.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, clear.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard tropes, noisy movement, ambiguous controls and
loading experiences that hide whether an action is actually in progress.

## Design Principles

- Make the current task and system status apparent at a glance.
- Preserve user context and entered information while data is loading.
- Use compact, familiar product patterns that work under event-day pressure.
- Confirm server-authoritative outcomes clearly and immediately.
- Prefer quiet feedback over disruptive or ornamental motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA. All loading and error states must be conveyed with text in
addition to color, remain usable with keyboard navigation and respect reduced
motion preferences.
